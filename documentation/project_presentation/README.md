## Presentation Highlights for Evaluators

- **Slide 1–3:** Problem Statement — Weight, wind shear, and multi-motor complexity of conventional solar panel trackers.
- **Slide 4–6:** Proposed Solution — "Rotate the Cells, Not the Panel" mechanism.
- **Slide 7–9:** Mechanical Transmission — Single NEMA 17 motor, central worm shaft, and 8 parallel shafts.
- **Slide 10–12:** Embedded Control & IoT Monitoring — STM32/ESP32, LDR sensor array, and live web dashboard.
- **Slide 13–15:** AI Predictive Maintenance & Energy Analytics — Net gain formula, motor current signature monitoring, and gear backlash estimation.
- **Slide 16–18:** Prototype Testing, Results & Future Applications.
